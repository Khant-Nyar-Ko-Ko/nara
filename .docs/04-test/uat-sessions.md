- All four read news mainly in English; the Test Plan asked for at least 3 mainly-Thai readers (not met — see [uat-results.md](uat-results.md) limits).
- Times are seconds. "—" = not recorded in the session.

## Session notes (interview + moderator)

### U02
- **Liked:** "I really liked how clean and fast it is. Usually when I check Thai news on Facebook or news websites, there are so many ads and distracting videos. With NaraNews, all the headlines are in one neat list right inside Chrome, and getting the email digest on my phone … is super convenient …"
- **Stuck:** "… during the second task, I paused because some of the Thai headlines used formal words I didn't know, and I wasn't sure if clicking a headline would translate it or open an all-Thai webpage. I looked around the popup for a language switch before realizing I could just use Chrome's right-click translate."
- **Keep using?** "Yes … If you add a one-click English or Burmese translate toggle in the future, it would be 10 out of 10 …"
- **Moderator:** Severity 1 (minor UX friction): paused ~8 s in T2 looking for a language switch in the popup, then used Chrome translate. Completed all tasks without help. T5 success was not marked in the record.

### U03
- **Liked:** "… I appreciate anything that cuts out the clutter. Regular Thai news websites have so many pop-ups and flashing ads … Having the headlines in one simple list and getting an email digest on my phone … is genuinely practical."
- **Stuck:** "… the font inside the extension window is a little small for older eyes, and zooming my browser didn't make the popup text much bigger. Second, since I don't read Thai fluently, I had to use Chrome's translate tool on the popup … a one-click English or Burmese translation button would be a game changer."
- **Keep using?** "Yes, I would. It saves me time every morning …"
- **Moderator:** Severity 1 (minor usability / accessibility): slight difficulty reading the small popup font. Completed all tasks without help. T5 success was not marked in the record.

### U04
- **Liked:** "… NaraNews puts all the Thai headlines in one clean spot without distracting ads, and the email digest is great when I'm commuting on the train or bus"
- **Stuck:** "I didn't really get stuck, though since I'm … not a native Thai reader, formal Thai newspaper words in headlines take me a second to read. Having a built-in English or Burmese translate button inside the extension would make it effortless."
- **Keep using?** "Yes, I would. It's simple, doesn't require any technical setup, and helps me stay updated … in under a minute …"
- **Moderator:** Severity 0 (no usability issues): completed all 5 tasks with zero errors. T1–T5 success was marked after the session on the moderator's confirmation (17 Nov).

### U05
- **Liked:** "The easy to use user interface and the fast responsiveness of the buttons."
- **Stuck:** "I didn't get stuck at all"
- **Keep using?** "I see myself using it for the foreseen future because of how easy it is to get the lastest news."
