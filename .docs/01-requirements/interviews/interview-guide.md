# NaraNews — Interview Guide (Pillar 1, Path A)

Goal: confirm or reject P1–P4 with real readers. Need ≥5 interviews (target ≥15). Ask only about **past behaviour**, never about the product idea.

## Who to interview
- Thai news readers who read Thai-language news at least weekly. Not team members.
- Start with the 4 survey respondents who opted in (Q10), then add more.
- ~15 minutes, in person or by call.

## Before you start (PDPA — rule.md)
- Say why: "We're a student team studying how people follow the news. About 15 minutes. Your name won't be written down."
- Ask before recording audio: "Is it OK if I record this? Only the team will hear it, and we'll delete it after we write notes." No → take notes only.
- Write an ID (R1, R2, …) in the log, never a name, email, phone or LINE ID.

## Questions (past behaviour only)
| # | Ask | Checks |
|---|-----|--------|
| 1 | "When did you last check the news? Walk me through it — what did you open first, then what?" | P2 scattered sources |
| 2 | "How many sites or apps did you go through? Why those ones?" | P2 |
| 3 | "Tell me about the last time you found out about big news late. How did you find out, and how late was it?" | P1 falling behind |
| 4 | "The last time you read a full article — how did you decide it was worth reading? Did you skip others?" | P3 effort |
| 5 | "The last time you were away from your computer, how did you catch up on news? Phone, email, LINE, something else?" | P1 / F3 delivery channel |
| 6 | "Have you ever tried something to fix this — an app, newsletter, notifications? What happened?" | Is the pain strong enough that they acted? |

Follow-ups that keep it on past behaviour: "When was that?" · "Can you show me?" · "What happened next?" · "Why?"

## Don't
- "Would you use…?", "Do you like this idea?", "How much would you pay?" — future/hypothetical answers don't count as evidence.
- Show the prototype or explain NaraNews before question 6. (Usability testing is a separate session.)
- Lead: "Isn't it annoying to check many sites?" → ask "How did that go?" instead.

## After each interview (same day)
1. Add one row to [interview-log.md](interview-log.md): date, ID, mode, length, at least one **verbatim** pain quote (Thai + English), pain #, and the past-behaviour fact behind it.
2. Note anything that **contradicts** a pain too — that is evidence, not failure.
3. Delete the audio once notes are written.

When ≥5 rows exist, run `/capture-requirement` with the log so P1–P4 cite interviews instead of (or as well as) the survey.
