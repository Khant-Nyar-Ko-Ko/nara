# DISCOVER survey — results (real users, Sep 2026)

Source: the team's Google Form (https://forms.gle/dJcYP3G8h3YZYB766), 7 respondents, all living in Thailand. Only answer counts are published; timestamps and email addresses are removed (rule.md PDPA). The raw export is kept by the team outside this public repo.

| Question | Answers (count of 7) | Pain |
|---|---|---|
| Are you currently living in Thailand? | Yes 7 | target group |
| How often do you feel like you're missing important news? | Sometimes 6 · Never 1 | P1 |
| How long before you find out about a major news event? | Same day 3 · Next day 3 · Up to date 1 | P1 |
| How many different sites/apps do you check to stay informed? | 1–2: 3 · 2–3: 3 · 5+: 1 (→ 4 of 7 check 2 or more) | P2 |
| How annoying is it to check multiple sites? (1–5) | 3: 5 · 4: 1 · 5: 1 (all 7 rate 3 or higher, mean 3.4) | P2 |
| Minutes per day reading news | <5: 2 · 5–15: 4 · 15–30: 1 | P3 |
| Would a one-line summary be enough for most news? | Yes 3 · Depends 3 · No 1 | P3 |
| Would you use a Chrome extension with one-line summaries? | Yes 4 · Maybe 2 · No 1 | F1/F2 |
| When not browsing Chrome, how would you want to be notified? | Chrome notification 3 · Email digest 3 · Don't need it 1 | P4, F3, F5 |

What it proves: the pains P1–P3 exist among 7 real readers in Thailand, and the delivery channels split between notification and email. What it does not prove: with 7 respondents it is not representative, and it is a survey, not an interview — see [interviews/interview-log.md](interviews/interview-log.md).
