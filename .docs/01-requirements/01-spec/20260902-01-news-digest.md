# Requirement Spec — News Digest (Fetch, Summarize, Deliver)

Date: 2026-09-02
Source interview(s): None conducted yet. Pain points below are the team's own hypothesis / internal brainstorm from the product pivot discussion, not yet validated by any outside interview, survey, or user session.

## 1. Problem & Users

Actors affected: general news readers in Thailand (including MFU-area students) who currently follow Thai news by visiting multiple news websites directly in a browser. The team believes (hypothesis, not yet interview-validated) these readers face three related problems when trying to stay informed: content is spread across many separate sites, they fall behind on the news because staying current requires actively re-checking those sites, and reading full articles to find out what's relevant takes more time than these readers want to spend.

- P1: "Falling behind on the news" — Source: team's own hypothesis, not yet validated by any outside interview.
- P2: "Scattered news sources" — Source: team's own hypothesis, not yet validated by any outside interview.
- P3: "Takes too much time to read news" — Source: team's own hypothesis, not yet validated by any outside interview.

## 2. Functional Requirements

### F1 — Aggregate headlines from multiple Thai news websites [Must]
As a Thai news reader, I want NaraNews to fetch headlines from multiple Thai news websites into one feed, so that I don't have to visit scattered individual news sites to see what's happening.
Traces to: P2

### F2 — One-line summarized, clickable scrollable headline list [Must]
As a Thai news reader, I want each fetched headline shown as a one-line summary in a clickable, scrollable popup list — where clicking an item jumps me to the full source article — so that I can scan the latest news quickly and only spend time reading the full article when a headline actually interests me.
Traces to: P3

### F3 — Email fallback when not active in Chrome [Must]
As a Thai news reader, I want to receive a news digest by email when I'm not currently browsing in Chrome, so that I still stay up to date with the latest news even when I'm away from the browser.
Traces to: P1

### F4 — Follow-up on specific breaking news [Won't]
As a Thai news reader, I want NaraNews to follow up with me specifically when a story I'm tracking becomes breaking news, so that I don't miss major developments on topics I care about.
Traces to: P1

Note: the product owner described this feature in their own words as a "future plan, optional" item — exploratory, not committed for this DISCOVER-phase spec. Per product-owner direction, it is recorded as Won't-have this phase (see Scope) rather than Should/Could.

## 3. Non-Functional Requirements

No non-functional requirements are specified in this phase. Plausible candidates exist for F1–F3 (e.g., a maximum feed-refresh interval for P1/"falling behind on the news," a minimum number of distinct sources aggregated for P2/"scattered sources," or a maximum email-fallback dispatch latency), but none of the raw notes or product-owner answers this run supplied a concrete number, count, or percentage target, and per rule this agent will not invent one. These should be raised with the product owner as a follow-up (see end-of-run report) and added as NFR1+ once concrete targets are confirmed.

## 4. Legal Requirements

### LR1 — Purpose-limited collection and use of email address for fallback delivery
Before an email-address field is added to any schema, the system must record a written purpose and lawful basis for storing it (e.g., "used solely to deliver a missed-news digest when the user is not active in Chrome"). The email address may be used only for that stated purpose and must never be repurposed for marketing or any other use without separate, opt-in consent.
Section: PDPA
Traces to: P1, F3

### LR2 — Traffic/access logging for the news-delivery backend
Because NaraNews's backend (the service that fetches headlines and dispatches the email fallback) is a "service provider" under Thai law, every HTTP entry point it exposes must log timestamp (UTC), source IP, route/method, status code, bytes, user_id/session_id (if authenticated), and user-agent for each request, and retain these logs for at least 90 days.
Section: CCA §26
Traces to: F1, F3

### LR3 — Verified identity required before email dispatch
Because F3 sends email to a specific address tied to a specific user, the system must be able to map the session/token used to trigger that dispatch to a verified real identity (email verified at sign-up); the email-fallback channel must not be enabled from an unverified or anonymous session.
Section: CCA §26
Traces to: F3

### LR4 — Recorded consent to Terms of Service / Privacy Policy for email fallback
Because enabling F3 requires collecting and storing an email address, when the user clicks "I agree" to the Terms of Service or Privacy Policy in order to turn on the email-fallback feature, the system must record user_id, authenticated session, document_version_hash, timestamp, IP, and the exact button text clicked. A bare `accepted=true` flag is not sufficient evidence of consent/intent.
Section: ETA §9/26/28
Traces to: F3

## 5. Scope

### In scope (this phase)
- F1 — Aggregate headlines from multiple Thai news websites [Must]
- F2 — One-line summarized, clickable scrollable headline list [Must]
- F3 — Email fallback when not active in Chrome [Must]

### Out of scope (Won't-have)
- F4 — Follow-up on specific breaking news — product owner described this as a "future plan, optional" idea, not planned for this DISCOVER-phase spec; revisit in a later phase.

### Core workflow (built end-to-end this phase)
News Digest Delivery: (1) The backend periodically fetches headlines from multiple Thai news websites (F1). (2) Each fetched headline is summarized into a one-line entry and shown as a clickable, scrollable list in the Chrome popup while the user is actively browsing in Chrome; clicking an entry jumps to the full source article (F2). (3) If the user is not currently active in Chrome, the same digest is instead delivered by email so the user still receives it (F3).
Built entirely from: F1, F2, F3 (all Must)
