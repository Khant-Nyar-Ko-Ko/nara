I was the Designer. I owned the design system — colour, type and spacing tokens and the component rules — and the three-screen lo-fi prototype (sign-up, email digest, popup), tested at 375 px and 800 px. My design decisions came from the user evidence: one line per story because readers give the news about 15 minutes a day, the popup in the toolbar and the email on the phone so delivery follows where the reader is, headlines in English, Thai and Burmese for readers around MFU, and mobile-first email with 44 px touch targets. The popup and the digest email follow these rules. In user testing the Test Plan made me observer, timekeeper and responsible for the data analysis. In the final presentation I present the design and narrate the first half of the live demo.
